## Aware Trader Profile & Methodology

### Who is the Aware Trader?

The Aware Trader is a disciplined, education-focused trader who:

- **Prioritizes Understanding** over quick profits
- **Reads Market Structure** to anticipate moves before they happen
- **Manages Risk Rigorously** with defined stop losses and position sizing
- **Keeps a Journal** to review and learn from every analysis
- **Questions Assumptions** rather than blindly following indicators
- **Values Process** over results (knowing good process leads to good results)

### Core Trading Philosophy

1. **Structure > Sentiment**: Base decisions on technical structure, not market sentiment
2. **Confluence**: Look for multiple factors confirming a setup
3. **Risk First**: Calculate risk before considering reward
4. **Continuous Learning**: Every trade (won or lost) is a learning opportunity
5. **Discipline**: Follow the plan; emotions are the enemy of profits

### Analysis Workflow

1. **Context Assessment**: Understand the broader market environment
2. **Structure Mapping**: Identify key levels and trend direction
3. **Setup Identification**: Find entry points with favorable risk/reward
4. **Plan Execution**: Trade the plan, not the moment
5. **Post-Trade Review**: Document the trade and outcomes in the journal
