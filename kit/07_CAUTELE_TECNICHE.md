## Technical Cautions & Risk Awareness

### Data Quality

- **Screenshot Resolution**: Ensure charts are legible and show recent data
- **Timezone Clarity**: Always confirm timezone when viewing timestamps
- **Indicator Verification**: Cross-check signal strength with multiple timeframes

### Analysis Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Trend Bias**: Don't force analysis to fit a preconceived bias
2. **Overfitting**: Avoid finding patterns that only work with specific parameters
3. **Historical Fallacy**: Past performance ≠ future results
4. **Confirmation Bias**: Challenge your own analysis with alternative scenarios

### Risk Management Reminders

- Always define stop loss BEFORE entry consideration
- Risk should be defined as a % of account (never exceed risk tolerance)
- No trade is worth destroying a trading plan

### Market Conditions to Watch

- **During Major News**: Volatility spikes, spreads widen
- **During Illiquidity**: Low volume periods may show false breaks
- **Market Overlap Hours**: Higher volatility and potential opportunity
